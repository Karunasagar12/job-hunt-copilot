# Apify

## What it unlocks

Apify powers optional public job sourcing. Sourced listings are added for you to review; you decide which ones to pursue.

## Cost

Apify has usage-based plans and a free monthly credit. Job Hunt Copilot includes a configured charge guard for sourcing. Review [Apify pricing](https://apify.com/pricing) and your configured limit before enabling it; actual terms can change.

## Get your key

1. Create or sign in to an account at [Apify](https://apify.com/).
2. Open account settings, create an API token, and keep it private.

## Connect it

In Settings, find the Apify card, paste the token, select **Save**, and then **Test**. Configure the sourcing locations and charge limit before turning on scheduled sourcing. Review suggested jobs before adding them to your application pipeline.
